Reed Stalker
Medium monstrosity, unaligned
Armor Class 14 (natural armor)
Hit Points 33 (6d8 + 6)
Speed 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
14 (+2) 15 (+2) 12 (+1) 3 (-4) 12 (+1) 6 (-2)
Skills Perception +3, Stealth +6
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
Languages —
Challenge 1 (200 XP)
Reed Camouflage. The reed stalker has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in marsh terrain.
Actions
Multiattack. The reed stalker makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
