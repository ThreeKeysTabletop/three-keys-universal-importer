## Rules content
This module bundles **no** D&D rules text. The one-click SRD import copies documents from the compendiums of the **dnd5e** game system
(by Andrew Clayton / Foundry Gaming, MIT code), which carries the SRD content under CC-BY-4.0. Only documents the system labels
`system.source.license = "CC-BY-4.0"` are copied; entries labelled "Unlicensed" or unlabelled are skipped. Artwork paths stay pointing
at the system's own files (not copied; their own licences apply, see the dnd5e system's LICENSE files).

The importer writes the following notices into every imported world (journal "Imported Content Attribution") and into each document's Source → Licence:

The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.
